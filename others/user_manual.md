# 🤖 MorphoBot User Manual

---

## ⚠️ Precautions

1. **Place the bot on level ground** with the wheels in the drive position to avoid jerks during power-up.
2. **Turn on the RC transmitter first**, ensuring all buttons and analog sticks are at their **zero positions**, for faster binding.
3. **Tighten all screws on the main chassis** after each flight to prevent mishaps.
4. **Ensure propellers are securely fastened** before powering on.
5. **Check battery voltage** — it should be between **14.8 V and 16.8 V**. If not, charge the battery using a standard LiPo charger by using the 4S settings to fuly charge the battery upto 16.8 V.
6. **Inspect parts for cracks** and replace any damaged components immediately.
7. **Ensure all wires are intact and untangled**, especially near the wheel assembly and propellers in drive and drone modes.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Power On the Transmitter
- Switch on the **RC transmitter**.
- Ensure all buttons and analog sticks are in the **zero position**.

### Step 2: Position the Bot
- Place the bot on a **flat surface**.
- Ensure the **wheels are in the drive position**, then power it on.

### Step 3: Initialization
- Do **not move the bot** while it initializes to allow proper **gyroscope calibration**.

### Step 4: Ready to Go!
- The bot is now ready to use in **Drive Mode**.

---

## 🛞 Drive Mode

- **Activated when:** `SB` and `SC` switches are in the **low** position.
- **Control Stick:** Use the **right analog stick**.

### Controls:
- **Forward/Backward:** Move the right stick **vertically**.
- **Turning:**
  1. Move the right stick **left/right** to choose turn direction.
  2. While holding the horizontal direction, adjust the **vertical** position to control turning **speed**.

---

## 🛸 Drone Mode

1. **Arm the drone** by pressing the `SA` button — the BLDC motors will begin spinning at low RPM.
2. **Throttle & Yaw:** Controlled using the **left analog stick**.
3. **Pitch & Roll:** Controlled using the **right analog stick**.

## 🔄 Switching Between Modes

### 🚗 Drive Mode → 🛸 Drone Mode

1. Press the `SD` button to enter **Gear Up Mode**.
2. Move the `SC` switch to the **middle** position. Wait for the bot to **morph** into drone mode.
3. Once fully morphed, set `SC` to the **highest** position to enter **Stabilized Flight Mode**.
4. With `SC` in the **highest** position, set `SB` to the **middle** and **highest** positions to enter the **Acro** and **Pos hold**  modes respectively. 

### 🛸 Drone Mode → 🚗 Drive Mode

1. Unpress the `SA` button to **disarm** the BLDC motors.
2. Unpress the `SD` button to enter **Gear Down Mode**.
3. Move the `SC` switch to the **middle** position. Wait for the bot to **morph** into drive mode.
4. Once in drive position, move `SC` to the **lowest** position to return to **Drive Mode**.
