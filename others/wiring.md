1. As seen in the image, the SpeedyBee is kept on the top plate so that all connections from it can be simply made to other components. The motor driver (L298N) and both PCB's are kept on the bottom plate and all PWM connections are made to the SpeedyBee.
2. The ELRS receiver is soldered directly to the SpeedyBee and kept on top. 
3. Further connections between the SpeedyBee ESC and the BLDC motors are made directly.
4. The DC motors are connected to the output of the L298N and the two motors on each side are connected to the same output (since they will always rotate in the same direction and speed).
5. Power is given by the battery using an XT60 connector to the SpeedyBee and 1 PCB. Wires are soldered from this PCB's power terminals to the other PCB and L298N.
